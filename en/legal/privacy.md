## Data controller

Maxime Lenne, publisher of maxime-lenne.fr. Contact: [hello@maxime-lenne.fr](mailto:hello@maxime-lenne.fr).

## Data collected

The site has no form, no account and no member area. It only collects personal data in the following cases.

### Contact by email or phone

If you write to me or call me, I receive the information you choose to share.

- Purpose: answering your request.
- Legal basis: your consent and pre-contractual measures taken at your request.
- Retention: 3 years after our last exchange.

### Booking a meeting

The "Let's connect" and "Book a meeting" buttons open Notion Calendar (Notion Labs, Inc.). Information entered when
booking is processed by that service under its own [privacy policy](https://www.notion.so/notion/Privacy-Policy-3468d120cf614d4c9014c09f6adc9091).

## Audience measurement

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures traffic without cookies and without collecting identifying
personal data. No consent banner is therefore needed.

## Hosting and logs

The host, GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), processes technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) to keep the site
secure and running. They are kept for the period set out in the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Recipients and transfers outside the European Union

Your data is only intended for me and the site's technical providers: GitHub, Inc., the host; Cloudflare, Inc., for
audience measurement; the email provider behind the contact address; and, for bookings, Notion Labs, Inc. These
companies are based in the United States: transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and, failing
that, by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies. Your light or dark theme preference is kept in your browser.

## External links

The site links to other sites (LinkedIn, GitHub, Notion Calendar…). Their privacy policy applies as soon as you open
them.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, write
to [hello@maxime-lenne.fr](mailto:hello@maxime-lenne.fr). You may also lodge a complaint with the French data
protection authority, the CNIL ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)). Under French law, you may also give instructions on
what should happen to your data after your death (article 85 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés).
